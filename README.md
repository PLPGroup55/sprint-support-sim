# Northstar Support Hero

i want to vibe code about this "The Northstar Sprint

A 1-week industry working simulation - one client engagement produces all three assessed deliverables as its natural paper trail.

The Client & Context

Northstar Retail Co. is a mid-size e-commerce company. Their support team is drowning in three repetitive ticket types: order-status questions, return/refund questions, and stock-availability questions. They've hired your 4–5 person pod for one week to build relief for their support team - not a finished product, an MVP that proves the approach works.

The Product: Support Deflection MVP




What it must do - reduce manual ticket handling for at least 2 of these 3 categories:1. Order status - "Where is my order?" / "Has this shipped yet?"

Returns & refunds - "How do I return this?" / "When will I get my refund?"

 Stock availability - "Is this back in stock?" / "Do you have this in a different size?"





How teams may build it - the tech approach is the team's choice, for example:

A chatbot or decision-tree flow that answers the question directly, without a human

A self-serve dashboard customers check before a ticket is ever opened

A backend script/automation that auto-tags or auto-resolves tickets before a human sees them

What must ship alongside the prototype:

A working prototype covering ≥2 ticket types, demoable end-to-end

A 1-page go-live readiness note: what works, what's known-broken, what Northstar's own team needs to pick it up without your team in the room
